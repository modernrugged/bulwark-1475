© 2025 BULWARK STUDIOS

HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Defend your castle from waves of enemies!

Survive as many waves as possible.

CONTROLS

MOUSE: Click to place towers

1-4 KEYS: Select tower type

SPACE: Start next wave

ESC: Pause game

TOWERS

[1] ARCHER (50G): Basic ranged attack

[2] CANNON (100G): High damage, slow

[3] MAGE (150G): Area damage

[4] BARRICADE (75G): Blocks enemies

TIPS

• Earn gold by defeating enemies

• Place towers strategically

• Don't let enemies reach your castle!
