BULWARK 1475

Build the walls. Place the cannons. Survive the siege.

About the Game

Bulwark 1475 is a medieval-themed castle defense game that combines strategy, puzzle-solving, and arcade action. Defend your island fortress from waves of attacking ships and enemy forts across a treacherous river. This SNES-era inspired game features alternating phases of intense cannon battles and strategic wall building.

How It Plays: The Four Phases

🏰 Castle Selection

At the start of each level, choose your home castle from 5 available fortresses. It will be instantly surrounded by protective walls.

🎯 Cannon Placement

Place your cannons strategically inside your walled castles. Your home castle provides 2 cannons, and each additional enclosed castle provides 1 more.

⚔ Combat

Manually aim and fire your cannons at enemy ships and forts. Lead your shots to hit moving targets while defending against enemy fire that damages your walls.

🧱 Building

Repair wall breaches using Tetris-like blocks. Enclose additional castles to gain more cannons. If no castle is fully enclosed, you lose!

Levels & Difficulty

Battle through 5 levels, each with 3 rounds. Each level features a unique island map with different castle layouts. Difficulty increases with each level:

  • More and faster enemy ships
  • Stronger enemy cannons dealing more wall damage
  • Longer and more intense combat phases
  • More complex terrain to defend

Survive all 15 rounds to witness the enemy commander's dramatic execution by walking the plank!

Controls

Mouse

  • Click to select castle (Castle Selection)
  • Click to place cannon (Cannon Placement)
  • Click to fire cannon (Combat)
  • Click to place wall piece (Building)

Keyboard

  • Q / E: Switch active cannon (Combat)
  • Arrow Keys: Adjust aim / Move piece
  • Z / X: Rotate wall piece (Building)
  • SPACE: Fire cannon / Place piece
  • ESC: Pause game

Legacy & Inspiration

Bulwark 1475 captures the spirit of 1990s arcade castle defense games with chunky pixel art, strategic depth, and fast-paced action. Inspired by classic SNES titles, it combines the tactical thinking of tower defense with the quick reflexes of shoot-'em-ups and the puzzle-solving of Tetris. Every round is a battle against time and enemy forces!
